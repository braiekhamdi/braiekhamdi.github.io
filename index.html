About

My name was officially changed from Hamdi Houichet to Hamdi Braiek following a judicial request. Per the ruling of the Tribunal de Mahdia dated August 30, 2022 (case no. 33217/2), my legal name is now Hamdi Braiek. Publications prior to 2022 may appear under the name Houichet.

I am an Applied Mathematics researcher and Associate Professor at ESPRIT (École Supérieure Privée d'Ingénierie et de Technologies) in Tunis, Tunisia, where I have been teaching and conducting research since 2020, and a researcher at the LAMSIN Laboratory. I received my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from the National Engineering School of Tunis (ENIT), University of Tunis El Manar, in 2019, with a thesis on variational and numerical techniques for medical ultrasound image analysis, prepared under the supervision of Prof. Maher Moakher and Dr. Badreddine Rjaibi. Before joining ESPRIT I taught at ENIT, ENSTAB, ISBB and ISAMM.

My research lies at the intersection of mathematical analysis, variational methods, partial differential equations, optimization and scientific computing.

More recently, my work has expanded toward machine learning and deep learning, physics-informed neural networks, neural PDE solvers and Bayesian optimization and their integration with mathematical modeling. The complete website, including my full CV, the research map, all publications, talks and the teaching portfolio, is at hamdi.braiek.com.

Research interests

  • Variational methods and total variation regularization for image restoration
  • Noise removal and inverse problems: Gaussian, speckle, multiplicative and Cauchy noise, with applications to ultrasound imaging
  • Fractional-order and variable-exponent models; nonlinear diffusion
  • Optimization algorithms and numerical methods: primal-dual schemes, fixed-point iterations, convexity splitting, topological gradient
  • Physics-informed neural networks, neural PDE solvers, Bayesian optimization and machine learning

Teaching

At ESPRIT I teach and develop courses in numerical analysis, scientific computing, optimization, machine learning and big data, from first-year engineering mathematics to graduate-level AI projects: Mathematics Fundamentals 1–4, Mathematics for Engineers, Numerical Analysis, Scientific Computing, Statistics, Estimation Techniques, Machine Learning, ML Optimization, Deep Learning, and AI Project.

Since 2021 I have also supervised more than twenty final-year engineering projects in data science, artificial intelligence, machine learning and computer vision.
